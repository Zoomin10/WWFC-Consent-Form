Privacy Policy

Wroughton & Wichelstowe Football Club (WWFC)

Last updated: 2026

---

1. Introduction

We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and store your information when you complete our digital player registration and consent form.

---

2. What Data We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Player details (name, date of birth, gender, age group)
  • Emergency contact details (names, phone numbers, email addresses, relationship)
  • Address information (postcode and house number)
  • Medical information and allergies
  • Consent preferences (data, photography, video)
  • Parent/guardian name and signature
---

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used for:

  • Managing player registrations
  • Ensuring player safety (medical and emergency contact use)
  • Club communication
  • Compliance with safeguarding requirements
We do not use your data for marketing without explicit consent. ---

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data under the following lawful bases:

  • Consent (for storing and using contact details)
  • Legitimate interests (club administration and safety)
  • Legal obligation (safeguarding requirements)
---

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is securely stored using cloud-based services (Railway/PostgreSQL). We take reasonable steps to protect your data from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

---

6. Data Retention

We retain player data only for as long as necessary for club operations and safeguarding requirements. Data will be reviewed periodically and securely deleted when no longer needed.

---

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties. Data may be shared only when necessary for:

  • Safeguarding authorities
  • Governing football bodies (if required)
  • Emergency services
---

8. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
To exercise your rights, contact us using the details below. ---
